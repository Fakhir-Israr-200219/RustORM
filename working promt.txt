### Lekin ek distinction important hai

**JOIN ka SQL/query-builder part complete hoga**, lekin **Relations system complete nahi hoga**.

Baad mein relations ke liye:

```text
belongs_to
has_many
has_one
many_to_many
```

aur:

```rust
User::posts
Post::user
```

ki generated schema/CLI support banani hai.

Aur `with()`:

```rust
User::find()
    .with(User::posts)
```

ye JOIN se alag **relation loading** feature hoga.
