Aur sabse important: aaj jo tumne PostgreSQL se actual Vec<User> nikala hai, usko disposable MVP code mat samjho. Ye architecture ka vertical-slice proof hai. Ab hum isi ko production-grade abstractions mein evolve karenge, na ke har ====================> Phase mein code dobara throw away karenge.
